Get playing

Set your tempo with the dial, tap along, or tap the BPM number to type it. Press Play.

Find your feel

Meter and Division shape your rhythm. Settings holds your sounds, accents, and skins. Tap a beat to cycle its emphasis.

Practice, your way

In Settings → Practice, add a count-in, change tempo gradually, or alternate audible and silent bars. When the click takes a break, keep playing.

Keep your setup

Save a preset to return to your tempo, rhythm, and sound next time. Settings and presets stay on your device.
